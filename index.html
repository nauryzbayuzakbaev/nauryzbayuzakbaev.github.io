Publications

Integro-derivation Dzhumadildaev algebras: from the algebra of polynomials

Ivan Kaygorodov & Naurizbay Uzakbaev

This paper introduces and investigates some properties of algebras constructed from the algebra of polynomials via derivation and integration operators using a process presented by Dzhumadildaev in an earlier work. In particular, we discover new classes of infinite-dimensional simple conservative algebras and describe the derivations of these algebras for ranks 1 and 2.

Classification of three-dimensional complex Leibniz dialgebras with one-dimensional annihilator

Tuuelbay Kurbanbaev & Naurizbay Uzakbaev

This work focuses on classifying three-dimensional complex Leibniz dialgebras. We present a complete list of these algebras when the annihilator of the associated Leibniz algebra is one-dimensional.
